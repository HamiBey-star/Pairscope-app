About PairScope

Last updated: October 25, 2026.

What PairScope is

PairScope is a free scanner for DEX trading pairs. It shows live pairs with price, volume and liquidity, scores each one with a Heat, Risk and Contract score, and lets you paste any token address to run a safety check. You can follow tokens and set alerts through our Telegram bot. It covers Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon and Avalanche.

Where the data comes from

Market data comes from DexScreener and GeckoTerminal. Contract security results come from the GoPlus Security API. We do not read contract code ourselves, and third-party data can be late, incomplete or wrong. The exact scoring rules are public on the methodology page.

What the scores are not. Every score is an automated estimate. None of them is an audit, a recommendation or a guarantee. A high score does not make a token safe. Always do your own research. Nothing on PairScope is financial advice.

What we do not do

  • PairScope is non-custodial. We never hold your tokens, keys or funds.
  • We will never ask for your seed phrase or private keys.
  • If you trade through the site, the transaction is built by third-party routers and signed in your own wallet.

How PairScope earns money

The scanner and the safety checker are free. The project earns from featured listings, which are paid placements. A token owner can pay in SOL for a listing of 1, 7 or 30 days, or get a 7-day listing by burning $PSP (see below). Every featured listing is labeled as paid placement and is not an endorsement. Before a listing goes live, the token must have a trading pair, meet a minimum liquidity requirement and pass our basic contract scan, whether it was paid for in SOL or by burning $PSP. These checks are limited and cannot catch every scam, and we can decline or remove any listing. The full rules are in the Terms.

The $PSP token

$PSP is the community coin of PairScope, launched on Solana. Burning 5,000,000 $PSP is an alternative to paying in SOL for a featured listing (see how burn listings work). Because listings can be obtained this way, the project has an interest in $PSP, so weigh anything we say about it accordingly. $PSP is not an investment and nothing here is financial advice.
