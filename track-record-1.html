    How entries are chosen and judged

    • Flagged: an automated job runs once a day. It takes the newest Solana pairs from the DexScreener latest-profiles feed, keeps those with liquidity of at least $10,000 and a Risk Radar score below 50, and logs the first 5. Nothing is hand-picked. The score, price and liquidity at that moment are saved with the date.
    • Checked after 7 days: the same job looks at the pair again and records one outcome. If no price data can be found, the entry stays pending rather than being guessed.
    • Rugged: liquidity fell below 5% of what it was when flagged, or the price fell 90% or more and liquidity is under $1,000.
    • Dropped 50%+: price is down 50% or more from the flag date, not rugged.
    • Mixed or flat: price moved less than 50% down and did not clearly rise.
    • Went up (miss): price is higher than at the flag date. We publish these as misses.
    • Pending: fewer than 7 days since the flag.
    • Entries are never deleted. A correction is added as a note on the entry.

    What this log cannot tell you

    • It only covers tokens we chose to record, not every token we scored.
    • A token can fall for reasons the score did not predict, and a flagged token can still go up.
    • How we scored them is explained on the methodology page.
