Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026. This policy covers the PairScope website at pairscope.online and the PairScope Android app, which shows the same website.

The short version

  • You do not need an account to use PairScope.
  • We never ask for your private keys, seed phrase, or card details.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • Most of what you set up (watchlist, alerts, paper trades) is saved on your own device.

What is saved on your device

PairScope stores a few items in your browser or app storage so the site remembers you. They stay on your device unless you use a feature described below.

  • Your watchlist, your alert settings and your paper-trading picks.
  • Display preferences, such as hiding a banner.
  • A random anonymous ID, created on your device. It is used only to link your Telegram alerts if you choose to turn them on.

You can remove all of this at any time by clearing the site data in your browser, or the app storage in your phone settings.

Reactions (votes)

When you tap a reaction on a token, our database saves the token, the reaction type, and an anonymous voter code. The code is made by scrambling your IP address and browser details with a one-way function, so it cannot be turned back into your IP address. We use it only to count one reaction per person per token.

Wallet lookup

If you paste a public wallet address into the wallet lookup, that address is sent to our lookup service and to blockchain data providers so the site can show public on-chain activity. You do not have to connect a wallet to use the lookup. Trades you choose to make happen in your own wallet app. PairScope does not hold your funds or your keys.

Telegram alerts

If you choose to turn on Telegram alerts, the alerts you set and your Telegram chat ID are stored by our alert service so it can send you messages. You can stop at any time by stopping or blocking the bot in Telegram. To ask us to delete your alert data, email us at the address below.

Hosting and analytics

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect the site. We use Cloudflare's privacy-friendly web analytics to count visits. We do not use advertising trackers.

Third-party services

PairScope shows data from, and links to, services run by other companies, including DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, GoPlus Security, block explorers, Jupiter and ParaSwap for swaps, Phantom, Telegram and X. When you use or follow a link to them, their own privacy policies apply.

Information you send us

If you email us, we keep your message and address so we can reply.

Age

PairScope is for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. No online service can guarantee perfect security.

Not financial advice

PairScope shows automated estimates, not audits or advice. See the terms for details.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
